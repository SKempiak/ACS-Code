Attainable performance at arithmetic intensity $I$ (FLOPs per byte of memory traffic) is bounded by
\begin{equation}
  P(I) = \min\bigl(P_{\text{peak}},\; B \cdot I\bigr),
\end{equation}
where $P_{\text{peak}}$ is the CPU's peak FLOP rate and $B$ the memory bandwidth \cite{williams2009roofline}.
