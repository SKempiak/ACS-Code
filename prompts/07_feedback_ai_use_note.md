# Prompt 07 — Review how I used AI and draft an honest AI-use note

Here's the thing I want you to be straight with me about. I used you (an AI coding agent) to
write most of this project's code, scripts and even draft the report. The rubric has an
"AI-use transparency" item, and my professor explicitly says polished AI code without
validated methodology isn't enough.

Look back over what we did in this project and help me write an honest AI-use note. I want
it to actually reflect reality: which parts you generated, which parts I had to change or
reject, and — this is the important one — at least one place where your suggestion was
wrong or misleading and how it got caught. Don't make it flattering; if the first version of
something was buggy or a benchmark would have misled me, say so. Then summarize it for the
report's AI-use section and keep `ACS-Code/AI_USE_LOG.md` as the running record.

Be concrete (tool/model, what was generated, what I verified or corrected), because a vague
"I used AI for help" note is exactly what the rubric is trying to discourage.

---
**What this demonstrates:** reflective, honest transparency about AI use — treating the
AI-use note as a real account of generation-vs-verification, including caught mistakes,
rather than a disclaimer.
